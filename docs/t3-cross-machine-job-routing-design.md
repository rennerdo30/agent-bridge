# Approved local job supervisor succession contract

Parent approved preserving job failover through an explicit durable routing grant and approved local protocol6/schema14 after reviewing this contract (AB-45842239/42240). This authorizes source changes only, without installed migration or daemon replacement.

Only a production broker acceptance path may mint a grant: an authenticated durable job runner reporting its own job, or its already authorized inline executor reporting for that same durable job. Capture the local store namespace, typed job principal, durable job incarnation, original recipient/binding/mailbox, project/control lineage, and the original report UUID. Neither a wire flag nor a job-prefixed conversation/body confers authority. Existing ambiguous history cannot acquire a grant retrospectively.

At succession, re-read the durable job and project/control policy after awaits and inside write admission. Verify the original job incarnation and local source namespace, authorized successor B's complete binding, and the current succession generation. Ordinary exact-name mail, concretely claimed agent-role copies, foreign-host copies and pinned remote sends cannot enter this path.

The proposed additive store records retain the original grant and every succession generation. Existing ownership/proof columns and bodies remain unchanged. A single transaction creates or verifies B's exact copy with the existing report UUID and switches the active route. A's old copy remains retained, becomes superseded rather than falsely marked consumed, and cannot be read/claimed/ACKed as active mail. ACK first versus succession first serialize on the same route: one consumer wins; a superseded claimant cannot acknowledge another recipient's generation. A previously consumed route never reopens. Duplicate/lost-response/restart/repeated takeover uses the same grant, UUID and route generation.

The source gate is **schema14 and local protocol6**, because intermediate protocol5 readers do not understand superseded-copy consumption fences. Their schema13/protocol5 lease declarations are incompatible; startup also checks their derived p5 endpoints before migration under the existing shared-home exclusion contract. Original schema13 records/proofs remain preserved, and ungranted records remain pending. Protocols3–6 and schemas11–14 are unreleased branch states. Pairing and the existing remote ownership capabilities remain unchanged because this grant is restricted to local-origin/local-mailbox job routing and is never accepted as a remote field. Remote copies retain their existing immutable recipient and receipt contract.

Before first broker acceptance, `JobManager.post` may retain a separate `reportRouting` draft in its already authorized durable job record. It records the actual producer owner, admitted store namespace, durable principal/incarnation, original recipient proof and a digest of the exact envelope. Recovery validates all of that evidence against current durable job authority before minting a mailbox grant. Caller message fields cannot create this metadata. A matching generation-zero original-owner read journal settles the original copy before fallback selection; an old or unrelated journal cannot settle another generation. Old drafts lacking this evidence remain pending rather than being inferred from job-prefixed names.

Schema14 adds `job_mail_grants`, `job_mail_succession` and `job_mail_retirements`. The selected-table message backup includes all three with messages, ownership, namespace and routes under one stable SQLite snapshot. Interrupted additive migration rolls back the new tables without altering schema13 rows/proofs; retry is idempotent. Explicit isolated restore adopts the captured namespace together with these ledgers, so retired A cannot become readable while B remains the active route.

Focused regressions will cover unavailable A to authorized B, unrelated project, replacement session, spoofed grant, foreign namespace/host, revoked succession after await, original incarnation replacement, A/B ACK ordering, lost response, restart and repeated takeover. Full runtime acceptance remains hosted CI. The new transfer admission capture also adds one callback to protected broker `startServices`; its exact normalized delta/hash needs a supplemental parent pin review before the separate baseline amendment.
