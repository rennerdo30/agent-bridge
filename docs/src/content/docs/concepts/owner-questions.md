---
title: "Questions to the owner"
---

Agents can ask the owner a question in **Waiting for you**. Open the question, review its context and reply in the composer. The response returns to the requesting agent; notifications direct attention to pending questions.

Questions are separate from tool approval requests. An approval authorizes a specific pending operation; a question asks for a preference, decision or missing information. See [dashboard approvals](../../approval-api/) and [decisions](../../decisions/).
